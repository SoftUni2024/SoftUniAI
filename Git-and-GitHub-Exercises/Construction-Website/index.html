Our story

Built around a better kind of dental visit.

Luma began with a simple belief: excellent clinical care and a genuinely comfortable experience should always belong together.

In 2011, Dr. Elena Marin opened our first studio with one treatment room and a determination to do things differently. No rushed conversations. No confusing treatment plans. No one-size-fits-all care.

Today, our team brings together different areas of expertise under one roof, while keeping that original promise at the heart of every appointment: listen carefully, explain clearly, and care thoughtfully.

Dr. Elena MarinFounder & Clinical Director
